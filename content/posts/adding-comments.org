Yeah - way back when, this blog supported comments. Never got that many but I liked that people had the option to chime in. I was using disqus and it worked pretty well although I didn't always get alerts on comments.

When I redid the blog and switched themes, even though the configuration was identical, things stopped working. I let it be but figured it was time to fix things.

I decided "no more disqus" - it was always a bit wonky and from what I've heard, they now put in a lot of cruft with respect to privacy and tracking.

Instead, I'm trying out [[https://remark42.com/][remark42]]. It's self hosted from a docker container which means everything is on something I control which means no spyware or tracking of commenters.

I also made a couple of other changes under the hood relating to the theme and hosting.

So, if you get a chance could you please leave a quick comment here or reply to a comment and I'd love people to chime in on future blog posts.
